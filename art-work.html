Commission process

  1. Start with an inquiry. Tell us about your horse, the style or size you are considering, and your ideal timeline.
  2. Share reference photos. Clear, well-lit images from several angles help capture markings, expression, and personality.
  3. Confirm the details. The subject, format, timing, and price are agreed before work begins.
  4. Receive a lasting keepsake. Your finished portrait is prepared with care for display, gifting, or remembrance.
